      COINS

      • Every match pays coins: showing up, rounds won, wins, perfect rounds, super finishes and a CPU difficulty bonus. Clearing the Election Campaign pays a big bonus.
      • Fighters can't be bought outright. Collect their shards from campaign chests, or buy single shards in the Shop. Coins also pay for upgrades and gold skins.
      • You start with 5 free Common fighters — Moshe Sharett, Yigal Allon, Tzipi Livni, Benny Gantz and Yitzhak Navon — so you and a friend can jump straight into Versus. 45 more fighters (including Ben-Gurion) unlock with shards.
      • Upgrades: every fighter you own can be ranked up to Rank 5 in the Shop. Each rank adds +5% damage, +5% defense and +50 HP. Ranks apply in every mode.
      • Campaign · 250 levels: fixed rivals that get tougher. Every 10th level is a boss. First clears pay a big bonus; replays pay 30%.
      • 🎁 Chest Store: buy chests with coins (150–1,600). Every chest starts with 3 upgrade taps. Bought chests upgrade at 45% per tap (campaign chests 30%), and Lucky chests have 5, 6 or 8 taps.
      • Chests: every 2nd campaign win drops a ballot-box chest (boss levels always do), and every 10th win gives a bonus chest 2 tiers better with 5 taps. Tap it 3 times — each tap has a 30% chance to upgrade it, then it opens automatically: Wooden → Silver → Golden → Epic → Mythic → Legendary. Chests give coins and shards.
      • ✦ Wild Shards: every chest has a 1% chance to drop one. A Wild Shard unlocks any locked fighter of its rarity — use it in the Shop.
      • Shards: collect 10 (Common), 15 (Rare), 17 (Super Rare), 20 (Epic), 25 (Mythic) or 30 (Legendary) shards to recruit a leader for free. You can buy single shards in the Shop with B.
      • Projectile ammo: 3 shots (+1 per upgrade rank, max 6). Mash them all, then they reload together — 1 second per shot — once you're empty.
      • Election Night Gold skin: win 3 matches with a fighter, or buy it for 150.

      THE RULES OF ORDER

      • Block by holding away from your opponent. Crouch-block (down + away) stops sweeps; jump attacks must be blocked standing.
      • Your Super meter fills based on the damage you deal; blocked hits give nothing. You can only hold block for 2.5 seconds before your guard breaks.
      • Crouch + Kick is a sweep that knocks down. Punch chains into more punches, a kick, or your Special.
      • Ability 2 types: Uppercut (rising anti-air), Counter (parry stance — strikes back if hit), or a second projectile/charge.
      • Gamepads: D-pad/stick to move, A punch, B kick, X special (hold down for Ability 2), Y super.
      • ESC pauses. M toggles music.
